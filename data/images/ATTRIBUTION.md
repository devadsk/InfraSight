# Image Attribution & Licences

All primary images in `data/images/` are synthetically generated by `scripts/generate_data.py` using Pillow, with exact programmatic coordinates and deterministic lighting parameters.

## User-Supplied Hardware Photos (`user_photos/`)
Technician photos added to `data/images/user_photos/` are original photographs taken by the developer of local hardware (routers, PC drive bays, breadboard LEDs).
- Rights: 100% original work created for this hackathon.
- Copyright & Privacy: Contains no corporate, customer, or confidential data.

## Synthetic Server Bay Renders
- Generated algorithmically using Python PIL.
- Ground truth coordinates and colors are established by construction in `scripts/generate_data.py`.
