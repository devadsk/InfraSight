# User Hardware Photos

Place your real-world photos (3 to 4 images) in this directory:
- Photo of a PC drive bay or external hard drive with LED.
- Photo of a home router indicator light.
- Photo of an Arduino or Raspberry Pi status LED.

Optionally tape a paper label (e.g. `SRV-004 / BAY-04`) in the frame to test real-world multimodal optical text and LED recognition.
